Touch

Description

The cue word 'Touch' together with a flat, open palm prompts the dog to move towards the hand and touch the open palm with its nose. The cue can be given while the dog is in any position.

The cue is useful for building a position relationship with hands, as a recall cue when used at a distance and to move the dog into a new position.

Equipment

  • Kibble

Hand signal

The hand is flat with the palm facing towards the dog.

The hand should be at held at the same level as the dog's nose, and at a distance that requires the dog to move towards the hand.

Training prompts

The cue is best taught by simply holding a flat open palm very close to the dog's nose and patiently waiting for the dog to choose to nudge the hand with its nose. The moment that contact is made should be marked, then a food reward delivered. Gradually increase the distance between the dog's nose and the open palm as the dog becomes more confident until the dog is moving short, and then longer distances, in order to make contact with the open palm. Build in variation by switching hands.
